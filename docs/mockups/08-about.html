About TechieBlog

The site, the author, and the stack it runs on.

TechieBlog is the personal publishing home of Ravi Rathore — a senior .NET architect based in Hyderabad, India. It's where I write deep-dive articles and multi-part series about the Microsoft stack: Blazor front ends, ASP.NET Core services, PostgreSQL data layers and MAUI desktop apps.

The site is also its own best case study. It's a Blazor Server application built with TrBlazeUI (a shadcn/ui-style Blazor component library), backed by PostgreSQL with Dapper and DbUp migrations, logged with Serilog, and tested end-to-end with Playwright. When an article here describes a technique, odds are the page you're reading it on uses that technique.

Everything published here — around 200 articles and counting — is written by hand, reviewed against running code, and kept updated as the platform moves. If you spot something out of date, tell me at ravi@techieblog.dev.
